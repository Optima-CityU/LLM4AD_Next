# Proposal objectives and key problems

Read [the objective-content-problem guide](../proposal-objectives/references/objectives-and-key-problems.md)
when defining or revising goals, work packages or research questions.

Turn the established evidence gap into a bounded overall objective, meaningful
sub-objectives and research work packages. Distinguish outcomes from activities.
Make scope exclusions, dependencies and the evidence for progress explicit.

A key scientific or technical problem explains a genuine difficulty in the
setting and a specific relationship to investigate; it is not a renamed task
or an assumed answer. Trace each problem to its objective, work package and
expected evidence. Repair inconsistencies within the requested author scope
without treating other passages as owned by inaccessible stages.

Keep the objective/problem explanation distinct from detailed method design,
while allowing the main agent to revise related Markdown for consistency.
Ask about consequential unresolved deliverables or scope choices, not every
internal transition. Do not invent methods merely to fill a future section.
