# Proposal assembly and integrated review

Read [the full-proposal review guide](../proposal-final-review/references/full-proposal-review.md)
when reviewing the assembled argument or preparing a deliverable.

For Phase 6, inspect S1–S9 in canonical chapter order, state/log and relevant
author requirements; for legacy projects review their existing sources without
migration. Check citation numbers against S4 (or the existing bibliography),
terminology, notation, figures and unresolved questions. Use the
[author-paced phases](author-paced-workflow.md) as conversational guidance,
without mandatory frontend stages or backend quality gates.

Trace the argument from context and evidence through the limitation, purpose,
objectives, research questions, method, evaluation and expected outcomes, and
back from outcomes through the plan to the evidence-based need. Identify broken
links, unsupported expansion, contradictions and duplicated responsibilities.

Make requested targeted corrections through the main agent, including related
passages when necessary for consistency. Preserve author facts and explain
consequential changes; do not return the author to a mandatory owning stage.
Describe findings with exact Markdown locations, their effects and concrete
next actions. Separate evidence, logic, method, citation and editorial issues.

Save a useful draft even when scientific or editorial issues remain. Summarize
findings and next options for author confirmation; a saved revision or
`draft_done` does not finalize it. Only the author's explicit **确认并排版** UI
action triggers platform conversion of the ordered chapters. The model does not
automatically generate/export Typst/PDF or require a compiler. A failed export
does not discard source or history. Do not introduce a readiness flag or quality
score as a backend save/export gate.
