# Strategy and budget

Adapted from AutoRebuttal's strategy memo, venue format plan, and budget
allocation workflow. See `/app/skills/autorebuttal-shared/ATTRIBUTION.md`.

Identify concerns shared across reviewers and answer them consistently. Choose
priority reviewers by severity, movability, and overlap with central claims.
For each concern select one primary move: correct a misunderstanding, point to
existing evidence, clarify wording, acknowledge a limitation, promise a
specific revision, or request an author-supplied result.

Choose the response structure from verified venue constraints. For a
per-reviewer form, allocate budget by reviewer and then by concern. For a
global form, reserve a concise shared opening only when it saves repetition.
Keep a safety margin instead of consuming the exact character limit.

Budget priority order: central correctness and evidence, reviewer-specific
questions, limitations and concrete revisions, then low-impact presentation
issues. A budget plan must state its units and whether limits are verified or
unknown.
