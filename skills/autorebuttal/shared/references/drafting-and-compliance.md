# Drafting and quality review

Adapted from AutoRebuttal's initial/human response style, response moves and
final review guidance. See [the adaptation attribution](../../ATTRIBUTION.md).

Write reviewer-addressed blocks as appropriate: acknowledge the concern,
answer directly, point to evidence, state a supported change or planned
clarification, and disclose any remaining limitation. Use professional calm
language without mechanical praise, score speculation or dismissive claims.

An entry should address one coherent concern or a tightly related group with
a scannable title. Preserve technical precision, define ambiguous terms and
keep shared facts consistent while adapting the framing to the reviewer.

Apply the research checks relevant to the request:

1. A complete response covers its substantive concerns without duplicate answers.
2. Reviewer-specific replies retain stable identities, not display labels as IDs.
3. Shared/global placement is consistent with the requested response structure.
4. Claims match their evidence and traceable references.
5. A placeholder is never presented as verified evidence.
6. The response does not claim the read-only submitted paper was changed.
7. Remaining author actions are explicit and useful.
8. The agent-produced `rendered_text` represents the intended final response.

A targeted correction does not require reconstructing a full baseline or
publishing a new strategy/draft/review bundle. Keep findings and author actions
separate from internal audit logs; scientific review is guidance rather than a
backend gate. Save or propose the requested response using the current exact
revision contract, preserving unrelated author edits and earlier outputs.
